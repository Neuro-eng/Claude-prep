# Domain 5: Stakeholder Communication & Lifecycle Management (14%)

## Discovery (before building)
Business goal, users, current process, data availability/quality, constraints, risk appetite, **success criteria and baseline**. Skipping discovery = classic wrong answer.

## Expectation setting
- Be honest: LLMs are probabilistic; accuracy targets, not guarantees.
- Pilot/PoC with measurable exit criteria; phased rollout.
- Translate technical trade-offs into **business terms**: cost, risk, time, value.
- Present options with recommendation and rationale, not just a single answer.

## Lifecycle
Discover → design → build/pilot → evaluate → **gated release** → monitor → improve → retire.
- **Change control**: version prompts, models, tools; review and regression-test before release; rollback plan.
- **Model deprecations/upgrades** are planned changes, not surprises.
- Ongoing review: quality metrics, cost, incidents, user feedback.
- Documentation, runbooks, ownership/RACI, training.

## Handling pushback
Resistance from security/legal/business → engage early, show controls and evidence, offer staged scope; don't bypass.
