# 4-week plan (6 Nov → 6 Dec), both exams

~1.5–2 h on weekdays, ~3 h on weekends. Adjust to your experience.

## Week 1 (6–12 Nov): Foundations + CCAO-F core
- Mon–Tue: Download both official Exam Guide PDFs; read `docs/00`. Book exam slots. Skim the Partner Academy CCAO-F learning path.
- Wed–Fri: `ccao-f/notes` ch. 1–4 (evaluation, verification, workflow design).
- Weekend: ch. 5–6 (governance, prompting). Flashcards `ccao-f.csv` (first pass).

## Week 2 (13–19 Nov): CCAO-F finish + CCAR-P start
- **17 Nov: new exam guides publish. Diff them against these notes.**
- Mon–Tue: ch. 7–9 (product/model selection, config, troubleshooting).
- Wed: CCAO-F practice: `quiz.py ccao-f -n 30`, log weak areas.
- Thu–Sun: `ccar-p/notes` domains 1 and 2 (36% of the exam).

## Week 3 (20–26 Nov): CCAR-P depth
- Domains 3, 4, 5 (evaluation, governance, stakeholders; 44%).
- Domains 6, 7 (models/prompting/context; developer productivity).
- Build something: a small RAG + MCP project with an eval set (even a toy). The exam asks *why*, not *how*.
- Weekend: `quiz.py ccar-p -n 25` twice; review every wrong answer.

## Week 4 (27 Nov – 6 Dec): Test-taking
- Full timed simulations: 60 q / 120 min (CCAO-F), 63 q / 120 min (CCAR-P).
- Last 2 days: cheat sheets, weak domains only, exam-day checklist.
- Run the **OnVUE system test** on the exact machine and network you'll use.
- Sit exams (CCAO-F first, CCAR-P a few days later).

## Rules of thumb
- Two passes of every flashcard deck before simulations.
- Don't leave a domain until you can explain why each wrong option is wrong.
- Score ≥ 80% on your own practice before booking the real thing.
