# Exam facts and the deadline problem

## Key facts (both exams)
- Delivered by **Pearson VUE**: OnVUE (online) or test centre. English and Japanese.
- Scaled score **720 / 1000** to pass. This is **not** 72% correct.
- Multiple-response ("Select TWO"): assume **no partial credit**.
- No negative marking: never leave a question blank.
- Closed book. No AI tools, no browser translation.
- Valid **12 months**; renewal via a free non-proctored assessment on the Partner Academy. Lapse = full exam again.
- Retakes: wait 14 days (after 1st fail), 30 days (2nd), 90 days (3rd). Max 4 attempts per rolling 12 months. Full fee each time.
- Must be 18+, government photo ID, name must match Pearson profile exactly.
- Registration needs a **company email on a partner-registered domain** (you have this via your partner access).
- Reschedule/cancel at least 48 h before, otherwise the fee is lost.

## Which exam first?
CCAO-F is generally reported as the easiest of the four. CCAR-P is judgement/consulting-heavy, with fewer obscure-config questions than CCAR-F. **Do CCAO-F first**; its material (prompting, governance, evaluation, model choice) overlaps with CCAR-P domains 4 and 6.
