Program overview
What is the Claude Certification program and who is it for? ​
The Claude Certification program validates that people at partner organizations have the skills to do real work with Claude, across three roles: Associate, Developer, and Architect. Certification counts toward your firm's standing in the Claude Partner Network, and it signals to customers that your team has invested in a real Claude practice.
Which certifications are available? ​
Four: Claude Certified Associate ($99), Claude Certified Developer – Foundations ($125), Claude Certified Architect – Foundations ($125), and Claude Certified Architect – Professional ($175). Prices are in USD before any partner-tier discount.
What's the difference between Architect – Foundations and Architect – Professional? Does Foundations upgrade to Professional automatically? ​
They're separate certifications with separate exams. Foundations proves an architect can build with Claude. Professional proves they can design and govern Claude solutions at enterprise scale. Foundations is the natural place to start and there's no formal prerequisite, so you can take Professional without holding Foundations. Foundations does not convert or upgrade to Professional automatically.
Which certifications count towards Claude Partner Network eligibility? ​
Exams that count towards Claude Partner Network eligibility:
Claude Certified Developer – Foundations, Claude Certified Architect – Foundations, and Claude Certified Architect – Professional
Exams that do not count towards Claude Partner Network eligibility:
Claude Certified Associate
Eligibility and access
Do I need to work at a partner organization to take an exam? ​
Yes. Certification is available to people at Claude Partner Network organizations. Registration requires a partner email address on a recognized company domain — personal email addresses will not work.
Can customers or non-partners get certified? ​
Certification is currently available only to organizations in the Claude Partner Network.
Is there a minimum age to take an exam? ​
Yes. You must be at least 18 years old to take a Claude Certification exam. Age is verified at check-in against your government-issued ID, whether you test online or at a test center.
How do I get access? ​
Go to Anthropic Partner Academy. You can browse to a certification directly, or start at the Claude Certification program page and open the certification you want from there. Each certification page has the exam guide, the prep courses, and the registration link. Go to Anthropic Partner Academy →
I'm getting "You aren't signed in with a partner company employee email." What do I do? ​
The email domain you signed in with is not yet recognized on your organization's partner record. First, check that you are signed in with your company email rather than a personal address. If you are and you still see the error, email partner-support@anthropic.com. Adding a domain requires a record change on our side and takes 7 to 10 days to take effect, so send the request before you plan to sit the exam.
Our company uses several email domains. Which one should my team certify under? ​
Certify under a domain recognized on your organization's partner record. Certifications earned on a domain that is not recognized — including subsidiary and regional domains — will not credit to your partner account. If your team spans multiple domains, email partner-support@anthropic.com with your parent company domain and the other domains that should roll up to it. We will update your record and confirm when it is done, which takes 7 to 10 days.
Registering, scheduling, and taking the exam
How do I register and schedule my exam? ​
Go to the page for the certification you want to earn, register and pay, and you'll receive an email with a link to Pearson. Log in there and schedule your exam, either online proctored or at a Pearson test center. See the step-by-step guide →
How long do I have to take the exam after I register? ​
Once you register for an exam, your registration is valid for 5 years. You can schedule and take the exam at any time within that window.
Which forms of ID are accepted? ​
You'll need to present a valid, government-issued ID at check-in, whether you test online or at a test center. See Pearson's proof of identification requirements for the full list of accepted forms of ID. See accepted forms of ID →
How do I change my name, address or phone number for my exam? ​
You can update these yourself in your exam profile — see How do I update my name in my exam profile to match my ID? in the Exam profile name changes and corrections section below for the full steps, limits, and timing.
What's the exam format and length? ​
All Claude certification exams use multiple choice and scenario-based multiple response questions. You have 120 minutes to answer them. Plan for about 135 minutes of total seat time, which includes check-in, instructions, and a brief post-exam survey.
What are the technical requirements for online proctoring? ​
Your computer and connection must meet Pearson's requirements. Check the requirements and run Pearson's system test before exam day. Check requirements and run the system test →
Which countries are eligible to schedule exams via OnVUE? ​
Candidates with a government-issued ID from Belarus, Cuba, North Korea, Russia, Syria, or restricted regions of Ukraine are not eligible for OnVUE online proctoring — you'll be turned away if you try to check in online with an ID from one of these countries or regions. You'll need to schedule and take your exam at a Pearson test center instead.
Note: Iran has its own, separate policy — see the next question.
I have an Iranian ID, or my primary residence is in Iran. Can I take the exam? ​
Following the suspension of General License G by the U.S. Treasury Department's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC), Pearson is suspending exam delivery for residents of Iran — both online (OnVUE) and at test centers — effective 12:00 AM Eastern Daylight Time (EDT) on September 8, 2026.
Note: If your primary residence is not in Iran, you can still test. You'll need to provide an ID at check-in showing that your primary residence is outside Iran.
My corporate network or managed device blocks the exam. What does my IT team need to allow? ​
Pearson publishes the full list of domains that must be reachable from your network for OnVUE exam delivery. Share it with your corporate network administrator, along with Pearson's system requirements. If the exam still won't launch on a managed device, you can take it at a Pearson test center instead. Pearson system requirements and domains →
Can I reschedule or cancel? ​
Yes. You must contact Pearson or access your online Pearson account to cancel or reschedule your exam at least a minimum of 48 hours prior to your appointment. Exams cannot be canceled or rescheduled less than 48 hours prior to your appointment. Failure to cancel or reschedule in time or failure to appear for your appointment will result in the forfeiture of your exam fee.
Note: When rescheduling your exam, be sure you receive an email confirming your new exam date. If you don't receive that confirmation, log back into your Pearson account to verify the new date appears in your profile — otherwise, your exam rescheduling may not have completed successfully and you risk forfeiting your exam authorization.
If I currently have a certification exam scheduled in English, can I switch it to Japanese? ​
Yes. To switch a scheduled English exam appointment to the Japanese version:
Log in to your Pearson account.
Cancel your current English exam appointment.
Schedule a new appointment and choose the Japanese version of the exam.
Note: You must cancel or reschedule at least 48 hours before your appointment. Changes can't be made less than 48 hours before it. If you miss this window or don't show up for your appointment, you'll forfeit your exam fee.
Pearson will email you a confirmation once your new appointment is booked. If it doesn't arrive, log back in to your Pearson account and check that the new appointment shows in your profile. If it isn't there, the booking may not have gone through, and you could lose your exam authorization.
Does cancelling my exam appointment in Pearson automatically issue a refund? ​
No. Cancelling in Pearson only cancels your scheduled date and time — it's designed to let you pick a new appointment, and it doesn't end your registration or trigger a refund. If you paid for your exam and no longer plan to take it, email certifications-support@anthropic.com to request a refund.
Can I use notes, documentation, translation tools, or AI assistants during the exam? ​
No. Exams are closed book. Browser translation tools are not permitted under the Pearson proctoring platform.
Exam profile name changes and corrections
What happens if my name doesn't match my ID at check-in? ​
Before your exam, you're required to confirm that your Pearson profile name matches your government-issued ID exactly — this requirement appears during scheduling, is included in your confirmation email, and is detailed on Pearson's Proof of Identification Requirements page.
If you notice a mismatch, update your name in your exam profile — see How do I update my name in my exam profile to match my ID? below — before your scheduled exam.
If you arrive at your exam with a name mismatch that wasn't reported to us in advance, Pearson will not allow you to test — and per their policy, your exam fee is forfeited and you will be required to register again and schedule a new attempt. A correction request submitted only after a denied-entry or no-show incident cannot be used to reverse that forfeiture.
Note: If you have questions about the timing of your check-in, or believe you were incorrectly denied entry, contact Pearson Support directly — they can review their proctor notes to confirm exactly when you were turned away.
How do I update my name in my exam profile to match my ID? ​
Your name must exactly match the government-issued ID you will present when you take your exam. That ID must be one of Pearson's accepted forms of identification — see Pearson's Proof of Identification Requirements.
Your name can only be changed once per year, so please check carefully before confirming. Enter it in Latin letters, including any accents, exactly as it appears on your ID. Include a middle name only if your ID shows one. If you have a single legal name, put a period (“.”) in the last-name field.
To update your name, please do the following:
Log into your Partner Academy account
From your profile, click Update Exam Profile.
Complete the fields.
When prompted, verify your login.
Confirm your details are accurate before saving.
Note: Your name locks for the year as soon as you confirm your profile — even if you were only updating your phone number or address — so double-check your name field before saving.
Phone and address: These aren't used to verify your identity and can be updated at any time. Wait at least 5 minutes between saves.
Note: If you haven't confirmed your profile yet, you may see a placeholder Anthropic address and phone number on your record — this is expected and won't affect your exam.
Note: Updating your name doesn't affect an exam you already have booked — your appointment stays in place. For timing guidance before you make this update, see How far in advance of my scheduled exam do I need to update my name? below.
Note: If the page says it can't find your exam record, you'll need to enroll in an exam before you can use this self-service update. If you can't open the page or save your changes, email certifications-support@anthropic.com.
I've already updated my name this year but need another correction — what do I do? ​
Your exam profile only allows one self-service name update per year — see How do I update my name in my exam profile to match my ID? above. If you've already used that update and still need a correction, request it from Anthropic Certification Support.
Please submit your name correction request as early as possible — see How far in advance of my scheduled exam do I need to update my name? below for timing guidance. These requests are reviewed individually on a case-by-case basis, and approval is not guaranteed.
To request a name correction:
Email certifications-support@anthropic.com from the email address your exam is registered with, with the subject line "Exam Profile Name Correction Request"
Provide your First and Last name exactly as it appears on your ID — clearly label which is which
Provide the reason for your name correction request
Use Latin characters only
How far in advance of my scheduled exam do I need to update my name? ​
It depends on how you're updating it.
Self-service exam profile update: For OnVUE (online proctored) exams, updates sync in near-real time. For test center appointments, updates sync only once per day — update your name at least 24 hours in advance of a test center appointment, or the update may not be reflected when you check in.
Name correction request to support: Submit your request at least 24 to 48 hours before your scheduled exam. Requests typically take 24–48 business hours to complete, and we cannot guarantee that requests submitted later than that will be applied before your appointment.
Note: Either way, if your name isn't updated in time, Pearson will not allow you to test — and per their policy, your exam fee is forfeited and you will be required to register again and schedule a new attempt.
Pricing and payment
Are there discounts? ​
Discounts follow your organization's Claude Partner Network tier. Registered-tier partners pay full price. Select, Preferred, and Global Premier partners receive 50% off, applied automatically at checkout. Through December 31, 2026, Global Premier partners receive a 100% discount on all certification exams; after that date, the standard 50% discount applies.
How do I pay? ​
You pay for the exam by credit card through Anthropic Partner Academy before you're directed to Pearson to schedule.
Can we buy in bulk or consolidate billing for our team? ​
Not yet. A voucher storefront — where partners can buy vouchers for their organization with discounts applied and track usage in one place — is in progress. Details will be shared when it's ready.
Did exam prices change? ​
Yes. Effective June 30, 2026, the price of Claude Certified Architect – Foundations increased from $99 to $125. Registrations completed before June 30, 2026 were charged the prior price.
My partner discount isn't showing at checkout. Why? ​
Select, Preferred, and Global Premier partners receive 50% off, applied automatically at checkout. Through December 31, 2026, Global Premier partners receive a 100% discount on all exams. If you're on one of these tiers and still see full price, your company domain may not be linked to your partner record. First, check that you're signed in with your company email rather than a personal address. If you are and you still see full price, email partner-support@anthropic.com. Adding or linking a domain requires a record change on our side and takes 7 to 10 days to take effect, so send the request before you plan to sit the exam.
Results and scoring
When and how do I get my results? ​
You'll see your score on screen at the end of your exam, whether you test online or at a test center; test center candidates also receive a printed score report. If you pass, you'll receive an email from Credly to claim your badge — this usually arrives within minutes for online proctored exams and takes a little longer for test center exams.
How is the exam scored, and what's the passing bar? ​
Results are reported as a scaled score of 100 to 1,000. The minimum passing score is 720 for all four certifications. Scaled scoring equates scores across exam forms that may have slightly different difficulty.
If I don't pass, will I find out which areas I was weak in? ​
Yes. Your score report shows your overall scaled score, your pass or fail result, and the percentage of items you answered correctly in each section of the exam. You can use the section breakdown to decide what to review before a retake.
Where can I view my score report? ​
You'll receive a copy of your score report via email. However, you can also access it any time by following the steps below:
Log into your Pearson web account and click "Log in"
On the Dashboard, under My account, click "View Score reports"
Click "View" for the score report you wish to view or print
Note: Score reports for exams taken before June 30, 2026 on ProctorFree are no longer available.
Retakes and recertification
If I don't pass, when can I retake? ​
Waiting periods increase with each failed attempt: 14 days after the first, 30 days after the second, and 90 days after the third. You can take an exam up to 4 times per rolling 12 months. This applies per exam, so failing one track doesn't block you from registering for another.
How much does a retake cost? ​
A retake is the full exam fee. Your partner tier discount applies to retakes.
How do I retake an exam? ​
To retake an exam, register for it again in Anthropic Partner Academy. As soon as your registration is complete, you will see a link in Pearson to schedule your exam. Pearson will limit the dates you can select based on the required waiting period between attempts.
How long is my certification valid? ​
12 months from the date you earn it. Certifications earned before June 30, 2026 under the previous 6-month validity have been extended automatically to 12 months from their original earned date.
What does recertification involve, and what does it cost? ​
You'll review what's changed since you certified and complete a free, non-proctored assessment on Anthropic Partner Academy. On-time renewal is free. If your certification lapses, you'll retake the full exam at the full fee to regain certified status. Full details will be shared before the first certifications come up for renewal.
Credentials and badges
What do I get when I pass? ​
You earn the credential and receive an email from Credly, our digital badging platform, inviting you to accept your badge. Once you accept it, you can share it on LinkedIn or anywhere else.
You can also download a PDF of your certificate from your Credly account. If you need help, see Credly's support article on downloading and printing your certificate.
Can my certification transfer if I change email addresses or organizations? ​
Yes. We recommend adding a personal email address to your Credly profile so you keep access to your badges regardless of where you work.
Moving to Pearson
On June 30, 2026, certification moved to Pearson for exam delivery and to Credly for digital badging. These questions cover what changed for people who certified or registered before that date.
I registered for an exam before the move to Pearson. Do I need to do anything? ​
We moved existing registrations over to Pearson in waves. If you had already registered, you should have received an email from us with your Pearson registration link — check your spam folder if you can't find it. If you didn't receive an email and you hadn't yet paid, register through the normal route from your certification's page in Anthropic Partner Academy. If you already paid and still aren't getting an email from Pearson, email certifications-support@anthropic.com — don't register again, as you would be charged a second time.
I passed or attempted Architect – Foundations before June 30, 2026. How does the move to Pearson affect me? ​
If you attempted the exam and did not pass, your earlier failed attempt is cleared and you can register and retake on Pearson with no waiting period. If you passed, your certification is valid for 12 months from the date you earned it.
I took the exam before the migration and never received my result. What now? ​
We're working through outstanding cases from before the migration. If you took Architect – Foundations and didn't receive a result, it's included in that work.
I passed before the migration to Credly and Pearson on June 30. Where is my badge? ​
All existing certifications have been transferred to Credly, and everyone should have received an email asking them to accept their badge. If you haven't received it, check your spam folder, then contact support.
Accommodations
What accommodations are available, and how do I request them? ​
Accommodations are requested through Pearson's accommodations process. Please plan to request accommodations 10 days or more before you plan on taking your exam. Request accommodations →
No Shows
What happens if I don't show up for my scheduled exam appointment (No Show)? ​
If you don't show up for your appointment, you forfeit the exam fee. To take the exam again, you'll need to purchase a new attempt.
Appeals
I disagree with a decision about my certification. Can I appeal? ​
Yes. You can appeal within 14 days of the date we notify you of the decision, or from your exam date for a results concern. Submit your appeal to Pearson. A disputed question does not by itself change a pass or fail result, and reporting an issue never counts against you or affects your result.
Support
Where do I go for help? ​
For exam support — including registration, scheduling, proctoring, and exam-day issues — contact Pearson support. For issues with your digital badge, contact Credly support at support.credly.com. To add or roll up partner email domains, or if a certification is not credited to your partner account, email partner-support@anthropic.com; record changes take 7 to 10 days.
Exam content and prep
Is there a practice exam? ​
The practice exam available on the previous platform was retired in the move to Pearson. The exam guide includes sample questions that show the format and style of what's on the exam.
How should I prepare? ​
Start with the exam guide for your certification. It lists the domains and task statements the exam covers and is the authoritative source for exam scope. Anthropic Partner Academy also has prep courses — coverage varies by certification, so check your certification's page for what's currently available. We're adding prep content over time.
Is content available in languages other than English? ​
The exam and prep content are available in English and Japanese. Note that browser translation tools are not permitted during proctored exams.
Can my organization see who on our team has certified, or who is ready to sit an exam? ​
Partner Portal is updated weekly with your organization's total number of certifications. Partners cannot currently see a detailed breakdown. If your number looks inaccurate, it will be reflected at the next weekly update.
SME network
How do I sign up for the Claude Certification Exam SME Network? ​
Help shape Claude certifications. We're building the exams that will certify Claude practitioners worldwide, and we want the people who know Claude best involved. If you or your team have hands-on Claude expertise, sign up for our SME network to help define what the exams test, review questions, or set passing standards. It takes five minutes, and there's no commitment. We'll reach out only when a project matches your background.

Data and Privacy
What is Skilljar and why am I logging into it?
Skilljar is a learning management system that hosts our educational content. You're logging into it to access the Anthropic course materials. This separate platform allows us to provide interactive learning experiences, track your progress, and ensure you have access to all course resources in an organized way.
What information does Skilljar collect about my learning activity?
Skilljar collects basic learning analytics such as course progress, lesson completion status, quiz scores, and time spent on materials. This data helps us understand how you're progressing through the course and allows us to provide you with completion certificates. All data collection is focused on improving your learning experience, and is subject to Skilljar's Privacy Policy.
How is my Skilljar data different from my Anthropic account data?
Skilljar only tracks your learning progress within this course platform, while your Anthropic account manages your access to the Anthropic Console and/or Claude AI services.
Is Skilljar secure? Where is my data stored?
Yes, Skilljar employs industry-standard security measures including data encryption, secure hosting, and regular security audits. Your learning data is stored on secure servers with appropriate access controls. Skilljar is SOC 2 compliant and follows best practices for data protection to ensure your information remains safe and private.
How do I delete my learning activity or Skilljar account?
You can request deletion of your learning activity by emailing academy-support@anthropic.com or deletion of your entire Skilljar account by emailing support@skilljar.com. Your request will be processed in accordance with applicable privacy laws and our data retention policies. Note that some data may need to be retained for legitimate business purposes, such as compliance or security, but we'll delete all personal information where legally permissible.
Do I need an Anthropic account to access the learning content?
No, you don't need an Anthropic account to access this learning content. The course is hosted on Skilljar and only requires a Skilljar account for access. However, if you want to use Claude AI services after completing the course, you would need to create a separate Anthropic account at claude.ai.
